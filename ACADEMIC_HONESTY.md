FluentYTDL is open-source software licensed under the [GNU General Public License v3.0](LICENSE). This notice explains responsible academic use. It does not add restrictions to the GPL-3.0 license or reserve special licensing privileges for the maintainer.

## Responsible use

Students and researchers may read, run, study, modify, and redistribute the code under GPL-3.0. When project code, architecture, documentation, or ideas materially contribute to academic work:

- follow the rules of your school, course, journal, or research institution
- cite FluentYTDL and the relevant upstream projects
- distinguish your original work from reused or modified code
- disclose source-code assistance when required
- preserve copyright and license notices in redistributed source

Submitting this repository, a renamed fork, or lightly modified code as entirely original work may constitute academic misconduct under the rules of the receiving institution. The maintainer may provide repository history or authorship evidence in response to a legitimate institutional inquiry.
