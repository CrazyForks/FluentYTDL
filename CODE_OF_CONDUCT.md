# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the FluentYTDL community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience
- focusing on what is best for the community, not only what is best for an individual

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery, sexual attention, or advances
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- public or private harassment
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission
- conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the current maintainer through the contact method listed on the [SakuraForgot GitHub profile](https://github.com/SakuraForgot). Do not post sensitive reports or personal information in a public issue. A dedicated project contact address will replace this interim channel when available.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. The maintainer will respect the privacy and security of reporters and affected community members to the extent possible.

Enforcement may range from a private correction or warning to temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces, depending on the impact, pattern, and context of the behavior.
